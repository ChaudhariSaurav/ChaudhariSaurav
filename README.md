# 💫 About Me:
Quality Engineer with hands-on experience in solar module inspection, final testing, incoming quality control, and SAP-based production workflow. Skilled in performing Electroluminescence (EL) Testing, Flash Testing, Hi-Pot, Insulation Resistance, and Ground Continuity to ensure modules meet technical, mechanical, and electrical standards.<br><br>I specialize in:<br>• Final QC, module validation, dispatch approval<br>• SAP MM transactions for material issue/receipt<br>• Incoming material inspection using AQL/ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 sampling<br>• Documentation, traceability, serial/batch mapping<br>• Visual, mechanical, and electrical inspection standards<br>• Cross-functional coordination with production, store, and testing teams<br><br>Along with my quality engineering background, I bring a strong foundation in Full Stack Development (React, JavaScript, Node.js, SQL), allowing me to solve operational problems with a data-driven and technical mindset.<br><br>I am open to roles in:<br>Quality Engineering | Solar QC | Production | Inspection Engineer | Testing Engineer | QA/QC | Manufacturing Engineer
